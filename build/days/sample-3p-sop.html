Sample SOP: How to Open a 3P Auto Insurance Claim & Send the LOR

📘 What this is

A job aid on how to open claims with a 3rd party auto insurance, from opening the claim to drafting and sending out the Letter of Representation to the insurance adjuster.

❓ Why we do it this way

We follow the step-by-step process to ensure that everything is well-documented and done efficiently. Opening of claims is relatively the first step in the settlement process, so it must be done properly.

☎ Who is the Senior Caller?

A member of the firm’s team, under the supervision of a Case Manager, whose primary task is communicating with insurance providers.

🚗 Auto Insurance Claim

A request made to an insurance company for compensation of damages sustained after a car accident, or for representation or intervention on the insured’s behalf when they are liable for damages.

1. Opening the Auto Insurance Claim

⚠ Reminder: once the intake packet has been received, open the claim right away.

Before calling the claims department: go to the intake tab, look for the TAR and the MVA Questionnaire, and get as much information as you can about the accident.

📋 What the claims representative commonly asks

  • 3P Insurance Policy
  • 2nd 3P Insurance Policy
  • 1P Insurance Policy
  • Claimant Name
  • Date of Loss
  • Time of Loss
  • # of Vehicles Involved
  • # of Passengers in Client’s Vehicle (cite minors)
  • # of Passengers in Defendant’s Vehicle (cite minors)
  • Cross Street and State
  • Police Report (Y/N)
  • Police Department
  • Incident Number
  • Accident Details (what happened?)
  • Witnesses (Y/N)
  • Ambulance Transportation (Y/N)
  • Hospital Destination
  • Currently Treating? (Y/N)
  • Providers providing treatment
  • Injuries
  • Client’s Vehicle (Year, Make and Model)
  • Vehicle Drivable?
  • The Damages on the Vehicle
  • Defendant’s Name, Vehicle Year, Make and Model
  • Second Defendant’s Name, Vehicle Year, Make and Model

1. Opening the Auto Insurance Claim: the Call

⚠ Disclaimer: some law firms do not give out any information on the call, whether the claim is 1P or 3P. Always follow your firm’s own policy on what may be shared.

✅ What you may give out during the call

  • Client’s name (do not give the middle or second name)
  • Date of Loss
  • Cross street and state
  • Police report and police department
  • No. of passengers in the involved vehicles
  • Brief description of the accident
  • Witness
  • Ambulance transport and name of hospital
  • Reported injuries
  • Always YES to treatment status
  • 1P vehicle Year / Make / Model
  • 1P vehicle drivable or not
  • Damage of the 1P vehicle
  • 3P insured’s complete details

📞 Call the claims department and open the claim

Once done, get all the necessary information: the claim number, the adjuster’s name, and the adjuster’s phone and fax number, and mailing address. You are all set!

2. Generating the 3P LOR and Drafting It

1. Generate the 3P LOR

  • Go to the Insurance-Auto tab and look for the 3P section.
  • Supply all missing information that you gathered from the 3P claim.
  • Look for the 3P LOR Docgen, choose 3P LOR for an Individual and click generate / regenerate (for the latest version of the letter).
  • Download.

2. Draft the LOR: first part

  • The highlighted parts of the letter are the fields to update / check.
  • Take note of how you will send the LOR (facsimile, email, certified mail or first class mail).
  • Add “S” if more than 1 passenger; remove “(S)” if only one client.
  • Add the names in the clients field if there are more; remove if only one client.

3. The adjuster’s details

  • Make sure all the information is there: the adjuster’s name, phone, fax and address.
  • Address the letter to the adjuster’s last name.
  • Add the initials in lower case.

4. Finish, name and save

  • Select all (CTRL + A) and remove the highlight: keep the letter as professional as possible.
  • Rename: INS - 3P (Insurance Name) LOR (Date) (Your Name), e.g. INS - 3P Geico LOR 2019.3.12 (Jairo).
  • Save as PDF, and save the DOC format too, with the same naming convention: a PDF and a DOC of your LOR.

3. Sending the Request and Following Up

1. Send it by fax e-mail

  • In your email, copy the fax #, remove the dashes, add 1 and @metrofax.com, e.g. 17023558660@metrofax.com.
  • Subject line: claim number - client’s name, e.g. Claim # 123456789 - Jane Doe.
  • Attach the letter (double-check the attachment by downloading it) and SEND.
  • Wait for Metrofax’s confirmation that it was sent successfully.

2. Upload and note it

  • Go back to the Insurance-Auto tab.
  • Upload the LOR in PDF and DOC format, and place a note.
  • Save.

3. Report and follow up

  • Go back to your task and report that you sent the LOR.
  • Do not complete the task yet: move it out 2 days and call the 3P insurance to follow up that the LOR was received.

4. After the LOR: liability status

  • Once the LOR is received, check the liability status.
  • Not established within 30 days of receipt: send the 3P adjuster a “Whether Liability is Accepted” letter.
  • Push for liability and follow up with the adjuster every 3–5 days (the Senior Caller’s discretion) until it is confirmed or answered.
