Objective

The purpose of this activity is to draft a Letter of Representation for both 1P and 3P. Your letter should be accurate, professional, and follow the proper format. Attention to detail is critical for this task, as it reflects your ability to communicate legal matters clearly and effectively.

Activity 1: Letter of Representation/LOR Drafting for 1P

Instructions:

  1. Use the Template:
    • Open the LOR template provided in the training GC.
  2. Input Assigned Case Details:
    • Replace all placeholders in the template with accurate information from your assigned case (case notes sent via DM).
    • Remove all brackets when replacing placeholders.
    • Follow the correct capitalization format used in the placeholders (ALL CAPS vs. regular text).
  3. Review and Verify:
    • Carefully examine the highlighted sections in the letter.
    • Not all highlighted items may require changes; however, each must be reviewed for accuracy and relevance based on your case notes.
    • Make all necessary edits to ensure the letter accurately reflects the case specifics.
  4. Attention Line:
    • If no adjuster is assigned: write "Attention: Claims Department".
    • If an adjuster is assigned: include the adjuster's name.
  5. Claims to be Opened:
    • For this activity, tick all types of claims.
    • (Note: In real scenarios, claims to be opened will depend on the client’s available insurance coverage.)
  6. Letter Date:
    • Use the current date as the letter’s date (i.e., the date the letter is drafted).
  7. Formatting and Grammar:
    • Ensure correct spelling, grammar, punctuation, and professional formatting, consistent with formal letter-writing standards.
    • Pay close attention to the proper formatting of the recipient’s address section.
    • The letter template is firm-specific, there is no need to reformat the body of the letter. Focus only on the highlighted sections.
  8. Saving the File:
    • Save your drafted LOR in your assigned Assessment/Activities folder.
    • Use this naming convention: INS – 1P Insurance Provider - LOR mm.dd.yyyy (VA's name)
